# Uchuu Trees: Conversion Record (2026-11)

The developer record of how the mini-Uchuu and micro-Uchuu tree data now in use were made and checked, written from the `uchuu-trees` campaign's dossier (`campaigns/uchuu-trees`, outside this repository; its `findings.md` is the full account and every figure below cites its evidence there). The user-facing statements are in the package READMEs, `convert/mimic-convert/CTREES-TO-LHALO.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`; this file holds what users do not need.

## What was wrong

- **The 2022 mini-Uchuu L-Halo files** (`Uchuu400_Planck_lhalo_binary.0`–`.127`, written in June 2022 by sage-model's `convert_trees_to_lhalo` with `fix_flybys` active) carried two defects, and `mini-uchuu-horizontal`, converted from them in October 2026, carried both.
  - **Flyby demotion at the last snapshot.** At each forest's snapshot 49 every FoF central but the most massive was made a satellite of it and had its `MostBoundID` negated: 3,108,878 of 33,585,418 halos; the FoF-central fraction falls from 0.857 at snapshot 48 to 0.769 at 49 (dossier E9).
  - **Shared `MostBoundID` in multi-tree forests.** Every halo in a later tree of a multi-tree forest took the `MostBoundID` of another row: 416,549,448 halos (28.7%) over all snapshots; 508,239,773 of 1,451,359,554 halos share one of 110,684,233 identifiers, at all 50 snapshots (E13, E24, E37).
- **Reach.** Only those two mini-Uchuu datasets. Clean: the 2021 mini-Uchuu Consistent-Trees ASCII source, all six micro-Uchuu datasets, the Shin-Uchuu horizontal dataset and its ASCII subset, the full-Uchuu file supplied, and (for the first defect, by its only evaluable indicator) the four Millennium packages (E9, E37).
- **Why no gate saw it.** Every gate compared two readings of the same data; none compared the data with its source. The validation skill now carries that lesson.
- **Effect on science.** With `sage16` at shipped parameters, the z ≈ 0 satellite-fraction offset of mini-Uchuu against micro-Uchuu fell from +0.1555 on the 2022 files to +0.0286 on the regenerated ones; z ≈ 1.4 and 2 were unchanged (+0.0096, +0.0108) (E23). Results computed on the 2022 files at z ≈ 0 should not be used.

## What was made

- **mini-Uchuu L-Halo.** Regenerated from the 2021 ASCII trees by `convert/mimic-convert/ctrees_to_lhalo.py`, which reproduces sage-model's conversion at commit `9218e11` except that it demotes no FoF central and gives each halo its own identifier (E21). It was first run at the 2022 particle mass, 0.0325, so that every record the defects did not touch stayed bit-identical for the comparison below (D6, E16); then, on the owner's direction that the Uchuu suite's single particle mass 0.0327 be used throughout (D19), `Len` was rewritten at 0.0327 from each record's stored mass with `convert/mimic-convert/lhalo_len.py`. On the two files compared, 0 and 127, the result is byte-identical to `ctrees_to_lhalo.py --particle-mass 0.0327` run directly (E45); the other 126 are covered by `lhalo_len.py check` (every `Len` reproduced at 0.0327, 0 mismatches over 1,451,359,554 records) and the per-file sidecars (input, output and tool digests; 289,761,548 values changed, 0 source mismatches). 1,451,359,554 halos in 25,843,142 forests, as before.
- **micro-Uchuu L-Halo.** The files sage-model wrote from the micro-Uchuu ASCII trees, with `Len` rewritten at 0.0327 the same way: 4,465,001 of 22,580,924 values changed, 0 source mismatches (E45). Their records were otherwise unchanged; every halo at snapshots 0–49 (22,580,924) matches exactly one source row by identifier (E37).
- **The two horizontal datasets** were remade from the rewritten L-Halo files by `convert/mimic-convert/convert_trees.py`; `h5diff` excluding `/halos/Len` finds 0 differences over 3,453 objects per set against the sets they replace (for micro-Uchuu, the previously published dataset; for mini-Uchuu, the set remade from the regenerated files before the `Len` rewrite), and the remade `Len` reproduce at 0.0327 (E45).
- **Particle mass.** `Len = roundf(float32(M_Crit200 × (1/PartMass)))` of the record's own stored mass. The 2022 mini-Uchuu files and the micro-Uchuu L-Halo files reproduce at [0.0325 ± 4e-9]; the micro-Uchuu HDF5- and ASCII-derived horizontal datasets already used 0.0327. Mimic uses `Len × PartMass` as a halo-mass fallback (`src/core/virial.c`), so outputs change where it is used.

## How it was checked

- **Against the source.** At every snapshot 0–49, every halo of the regenerated mini-Uchuu files matches its source row by identifier, no identifier is shared, FoF membership agrees with the source's host grouping (0 unexplained), and no host chain is broken or cyclic (E24, E33).
- **Against the 2022 files.** All 1,451,359,554 halos align: 1,034,055,825 identical once index links are translated, 409,782,207 differing only in `MostBoundID` (the second defect), 7,521,522 differing only in the fields the demotion touched, and 0 differing otherwise; no mass, position or velocity field differs (E24).
- **The converter's guard.** `convert_trees.py ingest` and `write_vertical_trees.py` refuse a source carrying the flyby signature unless `--allow-flyby-signature` is given (E12).
- **Release tests.** At the release head, with the installed data pinned by checksum (128, 51, 4 and 51 files OK), `make tests` passed on the default pair, on `halos-only`, `sage16` and `sage26` with `mini-uchuu`, `mini-uchuu-horizontal` and `micro-uchuu-horizontal`, and on `halos-only` with `micro-uchuu`; `make check-docs` and `make check-generated` passed (E47). The `mini-uchuu-horizontal` parity gate passed on 2026-11-22: 299,210,551 galaxies over 8 output snapshots bitwise identical in all 20 fields, fixed and dynamic (the 2022 files gave 299,185,269); `micro-uchuu-horizontal`'s gave the same 4,409,643 as its 2026-09-29 record.
- **Gaps.** micro-Uchuu's L-Halo files place 579 halos in a different FoF group from the one the source's host chain names (about 26 in a million; each of the 20 examined is a satellite in both); the cause is not investigated. The regeneration's peak memory was not captured. The Shin-Uchuu ASCII subset's completeness has no independent total. The vertical `shin-uchuu` package and full Uchuu beyond `mergertree_0.h5` were not scanned (E37, E21, E9). With `micro-uchuu` under `sage16` and `sage26`, 3 and 7 module integration tests fail; they fail identically on the micro-Uchuu data before the reissue, so they predate it, and they are not investigated (E47; backlog).

## Where things are

- **Data (Mac).** `/Volumes/Internal/data/uchuu/mini-uchuu/{mini-uchuu-lhalo,mini-uchuu-horizontal}` and `/Volumes/Internal/data/uchuu/micro-uchuu/{micro-uchuu-lhalo,micro-uchuu-horizontal}` hold the reissued sets; `conversion-provenance/` in each family folder holds the regeneration and `Len` records (`lhalo_len.py` sidecars, the converter workdir manifests). The superseded sets are on `/Volumes/Scratch/data/uchuu/{mini-uchuu,micro-uchuu}/*-old`.
- **Data (NT).** The owner copies the reissued sets to `/fred/oz214/dcroton/{mini-uchuu,micro-uchuu}/`, in the same layout as `shin-uchuu`.
- **Code.** The tools are in `convert/mimic-convert/` on `main`. The campaign's measuring instruments (`experiments/uchuu-trees/`: the flyby and shared-identifier scanners, the halo-by-halo matchers and comparison) are not on `main`; they are kept at the local tags `uchuu-trees/integration` (the signed-off integration head), `uchuu-trees/relen` (that head plus `lhalo_len.py`) and `uchuu-trees/dupscan` (the shared-identifier scanner, never merged), and in the `campaign/uchuu-trees/*` branches.
- **Upstream.** The defect is in sage-model's `convert_trees_to_lhalo` with `fix_flybys` active; the owner reports it upstream.
