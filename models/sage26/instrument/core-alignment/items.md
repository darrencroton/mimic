# Core-alignment set: the items

Notes for recreating each of the ten items of the frozen core-alignment set (`series`, `series.sha256`) after the campaign: what each patch changes, the SAGE26 behaviour it reproduces, why Mimic's behaviour differs, its measured contribution, how to apply it, and its provenance. Every patch is a measurement instrument applied only on a never-merged branch or clone; nothing here proposes a change to Mimic's core, to `sage16` or to the `sage26` package, and no item is assessed as a general core option.

## Conventions

- **Lines.** Mimic lines are at the launch base `66a3e9498561e7f2f2207267a94986e9b097b03c`, before any patch is applied. Between x01's base `b0071b073cb95c863ddcaab6f410e3d336cef22e` and the launch base, `src/`, the `Makefile` and `simulations/mini-millennium/` differ only in `MAX_MODULES` (`src/core/module_registry.c:43`), so the lines in those paths that the headers of 01 to 07 cite hold at the launch base; between x02's base `5e91c80866cedb56a23fb8e53d23bb775f40269d` and the launch base, `src/`, `sage_keyed_draw.h` and `sage26_determine_regime.c` are unchanged, so the lines 08's and 09's headers cite hold too. Every line this file cites was read at the launch base or in `R/`. `R/` is SAGE26 at `11b1ae851524baadb76cfd17e0b38f8a89c07224`, staged read only at `/Volumes/Internal/results/sage26-campaign/reference-src`.
- **Why Mimic differs.** The campaign decisions named are D1, D2 and D3, and for items 08 to 10 also D32, D46 and D47, as the Campaign Manager's answer, the package's source and patch 10's header name them. D2 and D3 are stated as the Campaign Manager stated them to the i02 node from the campaign's `decisions.md`: D2, timestepping is Mimic core, the `sage26` run files use Mimic's fixed `SubSteps` 10 (the dynamic scheme through the T2 gate's variant), and SAGE26's floored adaptive substep rule stays out of the package and the core, reproduced only by this set; D3, Mimic's core keeps double halo state, adaptive-Simpson cosmic time, its orphan `Len` and accumulator inheritance policy, its own output helpers (written `dT` and written halo values) and the mini-Millennium package's `PartMass` 0.0860657, and the package mirrors SAGE26's float precision only in its own reservoirs. The Mimic principles cited are those of `docs/VISION.md`.
