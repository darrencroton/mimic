# SAGE Shared Utilities

This directory contains helper APIs that are local to the SAGE model package. They are available when Mimic is built with `MODEL=sage26` and should not be treated as framework-wide conventions for other model sets.

If another model needs similar behavior, copy or reimplement the helper in that model package and reconcile the property names, units, parameters, and tests there.

Utility tests are registered in `shared/module_info.yaml`.

A path written `src/<file>:<line>` below is a location in SAGE26's repository at commit `11b1ae851524baadb76cfd17e0b38f8a89c07224`, never in Mimic's own `src/`; the headers write the same location `R/src/<file>:<line>` (the package README, *Departures from SAGE26*, defines the `R/` prefix).

## Available Helpers

- **`metallicity.h`** — `mimic_get_metallicity(gas, metals)`: safe metal mass fraction with zero/negative guards and a 1.0 cap (SAGE parity).
- **`central_link.h`** — FoF central index resolution: `mimic_find_fof_central_index` and `mimic_resolve_immediate_target_index`, the merger pass's target (`src/core_build_model.c:539-543`: a Type 1 targets the FoF central, a Type 2 its subhalo's central, and a target already removed is redirected once through its own central, with no fallback for an invalid link).
- **`time_parity.h`** — Substep timestep: `mimic_object_substep_dt` with strict initial-boundary handling (SAGE parity). The merger pass forms SAGE26's fused event time itself (`src/core_build_model.c:549`).
- **`sage_constants.h`** — Shared literal constants: `MergTime` sentinel protocol values (`SAGE_MERGTIME_UNSET`, the creation and central clock 999.9f; `SAGE_MERGTIME_CEILING`, the 998 cap of `estimate_merging_time`; `SAGE_MERGTIME_IMMEDIATE`, its -1 result), cold-gas yield threshold, metal-ejection halo-mass scale, virial-temperature coefficient, SAGE26's seconds per gigayear (`SAGE_SEC_PER_GIGAYEAR`, the literal `3.155e16` of `src/macros.h:37`, used by the cooling diagnostics), the hot-cooling return-path codes (`SAGE_COOLING_PATH_*`) that `sage26_calculate_cooling_budget` carries to `sage26_apply_cooling`, and SAGE26's `HYDROGEN_MASS_FRAC` (`SAGE_HYDROGEN_MASS_FRAC`, the double 0.74 of the H I diagnostic and the hydrogen caps).
- **`sage_merger_clock.h`** — SAGE26's dynamical-friction merger clock, `estimate_merging_time` (`src/model_mergers.c:89-121`): `mimic_sage_estimate_merging_time` returns -1 for a same-halo call or a failed mass, Coulomb-logarithm or particle-count (`Len >= 10`) guard, caps a clock at or above 999 to 998, and optionally reports SAGE26's locals. The satellite's and the FoF central's virial values come from the core's `Mvir` of their rows and its `virial_radius_for_mass` and `virial_velocity_for` at the snapshot's redshift. Used by sage26_initialise_merger_clock.
- **`sage_agn_physics.h`** — SAGE26's black-hole growth and quasar-mode wind, `grow_black_hole` and `quasar_mode_wind` (`src/model_mergers.c:346-441`): `mimic_apply_black_hole_growth` caps the accretion to `ColdGas`, floors negative cold metals, for `SFprescription 1` caps `H2gas` and `H1gas` each at the float 0.74 of the cold gas left (`:366-372`, under the site's own guard `SFprescription != 0 && != 2`, before the wind; the wind refreshes nothing, and the burst that follows every growth caps and refreshes them), runs the wind and then records the accretion; `mimic_apply_quasar_mode_wind` compares the wind energy with the cold binding energy and then with the original cold binding energy plus the binding energy of the wind host's selected pair, its CGM pair with `CGMrecipeOn 1` and the host's own `Regime` 0 and its hot pair otherwise (`:406-440`); both take `CGMrecipeOn` as an argument. Both optionally report SAGE26's locals, the selection included. Used by sage26_quasar_mode and by the burst transaction's post-minor-merger recheck.
- **`sage_sf_feedback_physics.h`** — SAGE26's star-formation and supernova-feedback commits, `mimic_sage_update_from_star_formation` and `mimic_sage_update_from_feedback` (`update_from_star_formation` and `update_from_feedback`, `src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:1119-1251`), each with its hydrogen cap for a prescription that tracks H2 (`SFprescription 1`: `H2gas` and `H1gas` capped, each on its own and strictly, at the float 0.74 of the cold gas left, after the star-formation stores, `:1128-1135`, and after the reheating debits, before the central's credit, `:1169-1173`; `mimic_sage_update_from_feedback` takes `CGMrecipeOn` and credits, caps and ejects from the FoF central's selected pair, its CGM pair with `CGMrecipeOn 1` and the central's `Regime` 0 and its hot pair otherwise, `:1175-1247`; optionally reported in `struct MimicSageHydrogenCapLocals`), the supernova code constants `mimic_sage_energy_sn_code` and `mimic_sage_eta_sn_code` (`src/core_init.c:102-103`), and `mimic_sage_validate_feedback_reheating_epsilon`, the one check of `FeedbackReheatingEpsilon` (finite and >= 0; SAGE26 registers it unchecked, `src/core_read_parameter_file.c:198`) that sage26_calculate_supernova_feedback, sage26_starburst_feedback and sage26_ffb_star_formation share. The one implementation of every star-formation and feedback commit: sage26_apply_star_formation_supernova uses it for disk star formation, sage26_ffb_star_formation for feedback-free-burst star formation (the feedback commit with the metallicity formed before the star-formation commit, SAGE26's `:1329`, `:1348`) and `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` for both burst channels.
- **`sage_fire_feedback_physics.h`** — SAGE26's FIRE supernova feedback for `FIREmodeOn 1` (`src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:743-792`, `src/model_misc.h:118-149`): `mimic_sage_fire_scaling` (the Muratov et al. 2015 scaling `(1 + z)^1.25 * (max(Vvir, 1) / 60)^beta`, beta -3.2 below 60 km/s and -1.0 at and above, zero for a non-positive velocity or a negative redshift, from the galaxy's own float `Vvir` widened to double; the high-velocity term written as the division `1.0 / x` SAGE26's object computes for its `pow(x, -1.0)`, the other two terms `pow`), `mimic_sage_capped_eta_reheat` (`capped_eta_reheat`: the loading capped at `2 * EtaSNcode * EnergySNcode / Vvir^2` in double, unchanged for a non-positive velocity), `mimic_sage_sn_energy_coupling` (`sn_energy_coupling`: `FeedbackEjectionEfficiency * scaling` capped at 2), `mimic_sage_fire_energy_ejection` (the Hirschmann et al. 2016 ejection `(E_FB - E_lift) / (0.5 * Vvir^2)` when `E_FB > E_lift`), `mimic_sage_fire_stored_redshift` (the redshift `ZZ[SnapNum]` of the galaxy's stored snapshot, refusing a `SnapNum` outside the run's redshift table with an error naming it and the table size, where SAGE26 reads `ZZ[SnapNum]` without a bounds check, at `src/model_cooling_heating.c:205`, `src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:745` and `:915`, and `src/model_mergers.c:694`) and `mimic_sage_validate_fire_mode` (the required `FIREmodeOn`, 0 or 1). Each optionally reports SAGE26's locals (`struct MimicSageFireLocals`). Used by sage26_calculate_supernova_feedback (the disk path), by `sage_ffb_feedback_physics.h` (the feedback-free-burst budget, `src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:901-972`, through sage26_ffb_star_formation) and by `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` (every burst, `src/model_mergers.c:690-752`, with the burst host's own `Vvir` and stored snapshot and no `MassLoading` write).
- **`sage_h2_physics.h`** — SAGE26's Blitz & Rosolowsky (2006) molecular gas for `SFprescription 1` (`src/model_h2_chemistry.c:48-290`): `mimic_sage_br06_stellar_scale_height` (the height, `+0.8` as the code has it, its fused `-0.23 + 0.8 log10` an explicit `fma()`), `mimic_sage_br06_molecular_fraction_from_hstar` and `mimic_sage_br06_molecular_fraction` (the pressure with the 0.1 Msun/pc^2 stellar floor, float `P0` and `alpha`, `pow`), and `mimic_sage_h2_radial_integration` (SAGE26's radial integration as `sfr_br06` calls it: 25 float annuli out to five scale radii with `expf`, the float `0.74f` annulus mass and accumulator, the float `h` and unit-macro roundings, no renormalisation, no write of `H2gas` on its early return and none of `H2DepletionTime_Gyr`), each optionally reporting SAGE26's locals; `mimic_sage_sf_prescription_tracks_h2` (SAGE26's guard of the commits' caps) and the validators of the required `SFprescription` (0 or 1), `H2RadialIntegrationOn` (1), `H2RadialNBins` (25), `H2RadialRMaxFactor` (5.0) and `StarburstColdGasOn` (1). Used by sage26_calculate_star_formation (its BR06 branch), the commits and the modules that load those parameters.
- **`sage_starburst_physics.h`** — SAGE26's collisional starburst transaction, `collisional_starburst_recipe` (`src/model_mergers.c:584-831`): `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` owns its whole sequence (burst gas, reheating, ejection from the host's own `Vvir` (fixed-epsilon with `FIREmodeOn 0`; with `FIREmodeOn 1`, the run files' value, the FIRE scaling from the host's own float `Vvir` and the redshift of its stored snapshot, the capped loading without a `MassLoading` write, the burst renormalisation and the energy-based ejection, `:690-752`, through `sage_fire_feedback_physics.h`; Used by sage26_starburst_feedback for both channels.
- **`sage_merger_physics.h`** — the pieces of SAGE26's merger transaction `deal_with_galaxy_merger` outside the black hole and the burst (`src/model_mergers.c:228-333`, `:456-570`): `mimic_sage_merger_mass_ratio` (the float `StellarMass + ColdGas` sums, 0 when both are zero), `mimic_sage_merger_target_disk_dominated` (the target's morphology before the addition), `mimic_sage_merger_burst_channel` (the burst's bulge channel from the ratio and that flag), `mimic_sage_add_galaxies_together` (the reservoirs, ICS with its accreted bookkeeping, CGM, the bulge and its two channels, the target's instability-bulge radius after satellite disk stars join its instability channel, and the histories into the target's bulge histories), `mimic_sage_make_bulge_from_burst` (the major-merger bulge), `mimic_sage_merger_remnant_radius` (SAGE26's static `calculate_merger_remnant_radius`, `src/model_mergers.c:135-212`: the energy-conservation remnant radius of the post-burst target and the satellite, with its float progenitor sums and `M_disk`, the sequential zero-radius fallbacks, the double energies and the `E_final <= 0` fallback; its three fused multiply-adds, `:158`, `:171`, `:204`, are explicit `fma()` calls; it optionally reports SAGE26's locals) and `mimic_sage_reset_bulge_radii` (`get_bulge_radius` with `BulgeSizeOn 0`, `src/model_misc.c:239-247`); and `mimic_sage_disrupt_satellite_to_ics`, SAGE26's `disrupt_satellite_to_ICS` (`src/model_mergers.c:845-902`: the float gas sums to the passed target's selected pair through `sage_cgm_routing.h`, chosen by the target's own `Regime` with `CGMrecipeOn`, an argument, the ejected gas, ICS and stars, the ICS bookkeeping at the event time, the black hole, and exactly SAGE26's cleared satellite fields). Used by sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption, and the channel by sage26_starburst_feedback's merger consumer.
- **`sage_sfr_history.h`** — SAGE26's six per-substep star-formation histories (`SfrDisk`, `SfrBulge`, `SfrDiskColdGas`, `SfrDiskColdGasMetals`, `SfrBulgeColdGas`, `SfrBulgeColdGasMetals`, ten bins each, `src/core_allvars.h:158-163`), held as sixty package-private float properties `<history>Bin<k>` because the property generator emits scalars only. `mimic_sage_sfr_history` maps `(history, bin)` to its element and is the only way modules reach them; `mimic_sage_sfr_step_bin` is SAGE26's substep bin (`src/core_build_model.c:514-515`); `mimic_sage_sample_disk_sfr` and `mimic_sage_sample_bulge_sfr` are SAGE26's disk and burst sampling (`src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:1058-1060`, and `:1325-1327` on the feedback-free-burst path, sage26_ffb_star_formation; `src/model_mergers.c:755-757`); `mimic_sage_reset_sfr_histories` is SAGE26's reset at the first-occupied update (`src/core_build_model.c:278-282`), which sage26_initialise_merger_clock applies at joining to a galaxy that stays a Type 0 or 1 (the histories are inherited, with no `init_repeat`, so a new orphan keeps them); `mimic_sage_average_sfr_histories` is the HDF5 writer's averaging into `SfrDisk`, `SfrBulge`, `SfrDiskZ` and `SfrBulgeZ` with SAGE26's divisors (`src/io/save_gals_hdf5.c:934-959`, `src/model_misc.h:49-58`), used by sage26_finalize_sfr_histories.
- **`sage_disk_instability_physics.h`** — SAGE26's disk-instability stellar transfer, `check_disk_instability` up to its black-hole and burst calls (`src/model_disk_instability.c:47-101`): `mimic_sage_check_disk_instability_with_sizes` applies the Mo, Mao & White (1998) criterion, adds the unstable disk stars and their metals to `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass` and `InstabilityBulgeMass` with no cap or clamp, updates the instability-bulge radius from the disk radius saved before the transfer (`:68-83`, a no-op with `BulgeSizeOn 0`), returns the unstable cold-gas fraction, and optionally reports SAGE26's locals; `mimic_sage_apply_disk_instability` is the same without the locals, and `mimic_sage_check_disk_instability` is the `BulgeSizeOn 0` configuration. Used by sage26_set_disk_scale_radius (the FoF central's synchronisation), the instability transfer, the satellite disk-star transfer of `mimic_sage_add_galaxies_together`, the burst's instability channel and the merger transaction's synchronisation and remnant assignment (sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption).

- **`sage_ffb_regime.h`** — SAGE26's feedback-free-burst (FFB) classification arithmetic for `EnhancedStarFormationOn` 1 (`src/model_regimes.c:151-159`, `:298-341`): `mimic_sage_ffb_threshold_mass` is `calculate_ffb_threshold_mass` (the Li et al. 2024 eq. 2 threshold `pow(10.0, 0.8 + log10(h) - 6.2 log10((1 + z) / 10))` in 1e10 Msun/h, its log term fused as SAGE26's object computes it, written as an explicit `fma()`; `pow`, which equals the `__exp10` SAGE26's object calls at every mini-Millennium snapshot redshift at `Hubble_h` 0.73 and every fixture redshift of the oracle's "The c13 Group", the only arguments the equality was established for: at micro-Uchuu's snapshot 3, `Hubble_h` 0.6774, the two differ by one ulp, the oracle README's "The c13 Group"), `mimic_sage_ffb_fraction` is `calculate_ffb_fraction` without its `EnhancedStarFormationOn` 0 return (the threshold first, 0 for `Mvir <= 0.0 || Mvir_ffb <= 0.0`, otherwise the eq. 3 sigmoid 0.15 dex wide, with `exp(-x)` in the object's form `exp(log10(Mvir / Mvir_ffb) / -0.15)`, which carries a NaN's sign as SAGE26 does), `mimic_sage_ffb_halo_uniform_bits`, `mimic_sage_ffb_uniform_from_bits` and `mimic_sage_ffb_halo_uniform` are SAGE26's static `halo_uniform` (the SplitMix64 hash of the 64-bit `MostBoundID` and the `(uint32_t)` snapshot, and its deviate `((double)(x >> 11) + 0.5) * 2^-53`, with no endpoint correction), and `mimic_sage_validate_enhanced_star_formation_on` refuses an `EnhancedStarFormationOn` other than 0 or 1, naming it (a required parameter with no default, loaded in `init()` by each reading module, where a missing key fails; SAGE26's mode 2 is not ported).
