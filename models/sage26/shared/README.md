# SAGE Shared Utilities

This directory contains helper APIs that are local to the SAGE model package. They are available when Mimic is built with `MODEL=sage26` and should not be treated as framework-wide conventions for other model sets.

If another model needs similar behavior, copy or reimplement the helper in that model package and reconcile the property names, units, parameters, and tests there.

Utility tests are registered in `shared/module_info.yaml`.

## Available Helpers

- **`metallicity.h`** — `mimic_get_metallicity(gas, metals)`: safe metal mass fraction with zero/negative guards and a 1.0 cap (SAGE parity).
- **`central_link.h`** — FoF central index resolution: `mimic_find_fof_central_index` and `mimic_resolve_immediate_target_index`, the merger pass's target (`src/core_build_model.c:539-543`: a Type 1 targets the FoF central, a Type 2 its subhalo's central, and a target already removed is redirected once through its own central, with no fallback for an invalid link).
- **`time_parity.h`** — Substep timestep: `mimic_object_substep_dt` with strict initial-boundary handling (SAGE parity). The merger pass forms SAGE26's fused event time itself (`src/core_build_model.c:549`).
- **`sage_constants.h`** — Shared literal constants: `MergTime` sentinel protocol values (`SAGE_MERGTIME_UNSET`, the creation and central clock 999.9f; `SAGE_MERGTIME_CEILING`, the 998 cap of `estimate_merging_time`; `SAGE_MERGTIME_IMMEDIATE`, its -1 result), cold-gas yield threshold, metal-ejection halo-mass scale, virial-temperature coefficient, SAGE26's seconds per gigayear (`SAGE_SEC_PER_GIGAYEAR`, the literal `3.155e16` of `src/macros.h:37`, used by the cooling diagnostics), and the hot-cooling return-path codes (`SAGE_COOLING_PATH_*`) that `sage26_calculate_cooling_budget` carries to `sage26_apply_cooling`.
- **`sage_merger_clock.h`** — SAGE26's dynamical-friction merger clock, `estimate_merging_time` (`src/model_mergers.c:89-121`): `mimic_sage_estimate_merging_time` returns -1 for a same-halo call or a failed mass, Coulomb-logarithm or particle-count (`Len >= 10`) guard, caps a clock at or above 999 to 998, and optionally reports SAGE26's locals. The satellite's and the FoF central's virial values come from the core's `Mvir` of their rows and its `virial_radius_for_mass` and `virial_velocity_for` at the snapshot's redshift. Used by sage26_initialise_merger_clock.
- **`sage_agn_physics.h`** — SAGE26's black-hole growth and quasar-mode wind, `grow_black_hole` and `quasar_mode_wind` (`src/model_mergers.c:346-441`): `mimic_apply_black_hole_growth` caps the accretion to `ColdGas`, floors negative cold metals, runs the wind and then records the accretion; `mimic_apply_quasar_mode_wind` compares the wind energy with the cold and the cold plus hot binding energies. Both optionally report SAGE26's locals. Used by sage26_quasar_mode and by the burst transaction's post-minor-merger recheck.
- **`sage_sf_feedback_physics.h`** — SAGE26's star-formation and supernova-feedback commits, `mimic_sage_update_from_star_formation` and `mimic_sage_update_from_feedback` (`update_from_star_formation` and `update_from_feedback`, `src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:1119-1251`), and the supernova code constants `mimic_sage_energy_sn_code` and `mimic_sage_eta_sn_code` (`src/core_init.c:102-103`). The one implementation of every star-formation and feedback commit: sage26_apply_star_formation_supernova uses it for disk star formation and `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` for both burst channels.
- **`sage_starburst_physics.h`** — SAGE26's collisional starburst transaction, `collisional_starburst_recipe` (`src/model_mergers.c:584-831`): `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` owns its whole sequence (burst gas, reheating, ejection from the host's own `Vvir`, bulge history sampling through `sage_sfr_history.h`, the star-formation and feedback commits through `sage_sf_feedback_physics.h`, the locked stars into the bulge and the caller's channel, the instability channel followed by its radius update from the caller's saved disk radius, the post-minor-merger instability recheck with its black-hole growth and burst, and only then the yield), and optionally reports SAGE26's locals. Used by sage26_starburst_feedback for both channels.
- **`sage_merger_physics.h`** — the pieces of SAGE26's merger transaction `deal_with_galaxy_merger` outside the black hole and the burst (`src/model_mergers.c:228-333`, `:456-570`): `mimic_sage_merger_mass_ratio` (the float `StellarMass + ColdGas` sums, 0 when both are zero), `mimic_sage_merger_target_disk_dominated` (the target's morphology before the addition), `mimic_sage_merger_burst_channel` (the burst's bulge channel from the ratio and that flag), `mimic_sage_add_galaxies_together` (the reservoirs, ICS with its accreted bookkeeping, CGM, the bulge and its two channels, the target's instability-bulge radius after satellite disk stars join its instability channel, and the histories into the target's bulge histories), `mimic_sage_make_bulge_from_burst` (the major-merger bulge), `mimic_sage_merger_remnant_radius` (SAGE26's static `calculate_merger_remnant_radius`, `src/model_mergers.c:135-212`: the energy-conservation remnant radius of the post-burst target and the satellite, with its float progenitor sums and `M_disk`, the sequential zero-radius fallbacks, the double energies and the `E_final <= 0` fallback; its three fused multiply-adds, `:158`, `:171`, `:204`, are explicit `fma()` calls; it optionally reports SAGE26's locals) and `mimic_sage_reset_bulge_radii` (`get_bulge_radius` with `BulgeSizeOn 0`, `src/model_misc.c:239-247`); and `mimic_sage_disrupt_satellite_to_ics`, SAGE26's `disrupt_satellite_to_ICS` (`src/model_mergers.c:845-902`: the float gas sums to the target's hot gas, the ejected gas, ICS and stars, the ICS bookkeeping at the event time, the black hole, and exactly SAGE26's cleared satellite fields). Used by sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption, and the channel by sage26_starburst_feedback's merger consumer.
- **`sage_sfr_history.h`** — SAGE26's six per-substep star-formation histories (`SfrDisk`, `SfrBulge`, `SfrDiskColdGas`, `SfrDiskColdGasMetals`, `SfrBulgeColdGas`, `SfrBulgeColdGasMetals`, ten bins each, `src/core_allvars.h:158-163`), held as sixty package-private float properties `<history>Bin<k>` because the property generator emits scalars only. `mimic_sage_sfr_history` maps `(history, bin)` to its element and is the only way modules reach them; `mimic_sage_sfr_step_bin` is SAGE26's substep bin (`src/core_build_model.c:514-515`); `mimic_sage_sample_disk_sfr` and `mimic_sage_sample_bulge_sfr` are SAGE26's disk and burst sampling (`src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:1058-1060`, `src/model_mergers.c:755-757`); `mimic_sage_reset_sfr_histories` is SAGE26's reset at the first-occupied update (`src/core_build_model.c:278-282`), which sage26_initialise_merger_clock applies at joining to a galaxy that stays a Type 0 or 1 (the histories are inherited, with no `init_repeat`, so a new orphan keeps them); `mimic_sage_average_sfr_histories` is the HDF5 writer's averaging into `SfrDisk`, `SfrBulge`, `SfrDiskZ` and `SfrBulgeZ` with SAGE26's divisors (`src/io/save_gals_hdf5.c:934-959`, `src/model_misc.h:49-58`), used by sage26_finalize_sfr_histories.
- **`sage_disk_instability_physics.h`** — SAGE26's disk-instability stellar transfer, `check_disk_instability` up to its black-hole and burst calls (`src/model_disk_instability.c:47-101`): `mimic_sage_check_disk_instability_with_sizes` applies the Mo, Mao & White (1998) criterion, adds the unstable disk stars and their metals to `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass` and `InstabilityBulgeMass` with no cap or clamp, updates the instability-bulge radius from the disk radius saved before the transfer (`:68-83`, a no-op with `BulgeSizeOn 0`), returns the unstable cold-gas fraction, and optionally reports SAGE26's locals; `mimic_sage_apply_disk_instability` is the same without the locals, and `mimic_sage_check_disk_instability` is the `BulgeSizeOn 0` configuration. Used by sage26_disk_instability and by the burst transaction's minor-merger instability recheck.
- **`sage_bulge_size_physics.h`** — SAGE26's bulge sizes for `BulgeSizeOn` 0 and 3 (`src/model_misc.c:239-424`): `mimic_sage_get_bulge_radius` is `get_bulge_radius` (mode 0 zeroes the three radii; mode 3 combines the merger and instability channels' radii, weighted by the channel masses, with the Shen et al. 2003 and `0.2 * DiskScaleRadius` fallbacks kept as double locals for the average), `mimic_sage_update_instability_bulge_radius` is `update_instability_bulge_radius` (Tonini et al. 2016 eq. 15 from the post-store channel mass and the caller's saved disk radius, with SAGE26's kpc round trip, then `get_bulge_radius`; mode 0 returns at once), and `mimic_sage_validate_bulge_size_on` refuses any mode but 0 and 3. SAGE26's four fused multiply-adds on these paths (`:345`, `:359`, `:366`, `:415`) are explicit `fma()` calls. Both optionally report SAGE26's locals. Used by sage26_set_disk_scale_radius (the FoF central's synchronisation), the instability transfer, the satellite disk-star transfer of `mimic_sage_add_galaxies_together`, the burst's instability channel and the merger transaction's synchronisation and remnant assignment (sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption).
