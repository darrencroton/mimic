# `sage26_starburst_feedback`

Ports SAGE26's collisional starburst, `collisional_starburst_recipe` (`src/model_mergers.c:584-831` in the staged SAGE26 source), with `StarburstColdGasOn 1`, `BulgeSizeOn` 0 or 3, `SFprescription` 1 (the run files' value, SAGE26's R2 and R3) or 0 (R0, R1) and `FIREmodeOn` 1 (the run files' value, SAGE26's R3: the FIRE supernova budget) or 0 (R0-R2: fixed-epsilon), for disk-instability triggers and merger events. With `SFprescription 1` the burst's star-formation and feedback commits cap the hydrogen (`shared/sage_sf_feedback_physics.h`), the burst refreshes it after its feedback (`R/src/model_mergers.c:798-803`, step 7b below), and the post-minor-merger recheck's black-hole growth applies its accretion cap (`:366-372`, `shared/sage_agn_physics.h`).

## The Burst Transaction

One shared function, `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` (`shared/sage_starburst_physics.h`), owns SAGE26's whole sequence for both channels:

1. The bursting fraction: the trigger itself for a disk instability (`mode 1`) and `0.56 * ratio^0.7` for a merger (`mode 0`), as SAGE26's code has them (`:600-604`; its comment at `:577` has the modes reversed).
2. The burst gas is the host's total `ColdGas` (`StarburstColdGasOn 1`), floored at zero, times the fraction; the stars are floored at zero (`:680-688`).
3. The reheated mass. With `FIREmodeOn 1` (`shared/sage_fire_feedback_physics.h`): the Muratov et al. (2015) scaling from the burst **host's** (the target's, SAGE26's `merger_centralgal`) own float `Vvir` widened to double and the redshift of its **stored** snapshot, `ZZ[SnapNum]` (`:690-703`), never the satellite's or the FoF central's, nor the current halo snapshot's; then `capped_eta_reheat(FeedbackReheatingEpsilon * scaling, Vvir) * stars` (`:706-710`, the cap `2 * EtaSNcode * EnergySNcode / Vvir^2` in double), and, unlike the disk path, **no** `MassLoading` write. A stored `SnapNum` outside the run's redshift table is refused (campaign decision D35): the burst writes nothing and `process()` fails. With `FIREmodeOn 0`: `FeedbackReheatingEpsilon * stars` (`:711-712`). Both are scaled down together when they exceed the burst gas, with no positive-sum clause (`:720-725`).
4. The ejected mass from the burst host's own `Vvir` (`:727-749`), not the FoF central's: with `FIREmodeOn 1` the Hirschmann et al. (2016) energy-based ejection `(E_FB - E_lift) / (0.5 * Vvir^2)` when `E_FB = coupling * 0.5 * stars * EtaSNcode * EnergySNcode` exceeds `E_lift = 0.5 * reheated * Vvir^2`, from the renormalised stars and reheated mass, the velocity widened to double (`:729-736`, `sn_energy_coupling` capped at 2); with `FIREmodeOn 0` the fixed-epsilon formula with the float `Vvir` squared in float (`:738-743`). Zero for a non-positive `Vvir` in both, then floored at zero (`:746-748`).
5. The bulge history is sampled for every burst, zero stars included: `SfrBulge[bin] += stars / dt`, `SfrBulgeColdGas[bin] += ColdGas`, `SfrBulgeColdGasMetals[bin] += MetalsColdGas` (`:754-757`).
6. The star-formation commit (`update_from_star_formation`), then `recycled_stars = (1 - RecycleFraction) * stars` into `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass` (one fused multiply-add, `:773`) and the channel the caller selects, `MergerBulgeMass` or `InstabilityBulgeMass` (`:770-782`). The instability channel is followed at once by the incremental instability-bulge radius from the caller's saved disk radius, the transaction's `old_disk_radius` argument (`:783-784`, `shared/sage_bulge_size_physics.h`; a no-op with `BulgeSizeOn 0`); the merger channel writes no radius.
7. The feedback commit (`update_from_feedback`), reheated gas and ejection going to the FoF central (`:787-791`).
7b. With `SFprescription 1` (`sf_prescription_tracks_h2`, `:798`), the hydrogen refresh, which is not a pair of independent caps: `H2gas` is capped at the double `ColdGas * 0.74` (the widened float compared with the unfused product, which is narrowed on a binding store, `:799-800`); then `H1gas` is overwritten, whatever it held, with the atomic remainder `ColdGas * 0.74 - H2gas` (one fused multiply-subtract in SAGE26's object, written `fma(ColdGas, 0.74, -H2gas)`, `:801-802`), and a negative stored result is floored at zero (`:803`). It applies no ionisation correction, so an `H1gas` the disk star formation reduced for ionisation is restored to the full atomic remainder until the next disk SF; every burst runs it, a zero-star burst included, before the recheck. After the feedback commit's float cap of the same cold gas, the refresh's `H2gas` cap binds only where that float bound rounded up above the double product, and then stores the same float.
8. For a merger burst whose ratio is strictly below `ThresholdMajorMerger`, the post-minor-merger `check_disk_instability` on the host, whose gas branch grows the black hole (with its quasar wind and, for `SFprescription 1`, its hydrogen cap) and bursts again in `mode 1` into the instability channel, that burst refreshing the hydrogen in turn (`:806-811`) and, with `FIREmodeOn 1`, running the FIRE budget with the same host's `Vvir` and stored snapshot (it receives the same parameter bundle).
9. Only then the burst's metal yield: retained in the host's cold gas (less the fraction leaving the disk) when the host keeps more than `1e-8` of cold gas and the ratio is strictly below the threshold, otherwise all to the FoF central's hot gas (`:813-830`). A ratio equal to the threshold is a minor merger in SAGE26 but gets no recheck and sends all its yield to hot gas.

There is no early return on a non-positive trigger, zero cold gas or zero stars. The star-formation and feedback commits are the package's one implementation, `shared/sage_sf_feedback_physics.h`.

## Channels

- `process_by_galaxy`: the disk instability (`UnstableDiskGasFraction > 0`, `mode 1`, the instability channel), after `sage26_disk_instability` and `sage26_quasar_mode`, as SAGE26's `check_disk_instability` grows the black hole and then bursts (`src/model_disk_instability.c:101-108`). `dt` is the galaxy's own `dT / num_substeps` (`src/core_build_model.c:516`).
- `process_per_event`: the merger event (`mode 0`), after `sage26_quasar_mode`'s merger black-hole growth. `dt` is the merging satellite's substep interval, carried in the event's `value1` (`src/core_build_model.c:579-581`, `src/model_mergers.c:285`, `:809`). The bulge channel is SAGE26's (`src/model_mergers.c:266-282`): `MergerBulgeMass` for a major merger (ratio strictly above `ThresholdMajorMerger`), and for a minor merger `InstabilityBulgeMass` onto a target that was disk-dominated before the satellite was added and `MergerBulgeMass` otherwise, from the flag the resolver carries on the target in `MergerDiskDominated` (`:251-254`). Every merger bursts, a ratio of 0 included.

Each channel passes SAGE26's `old_disk_radius`: the galaxy's current `DiskScaleRadius` for the disk instability, as `check_disk_instability` passes it (`src/model_disk_instability.c:106-107`), and the target's `DiskScaleRadius` for a merger, which SAGE26 saves before `add_galaxies_together` (`src/model_mergers.c:257`); no package module writes `DiskScaleRadius` between that point of the resolver's transaction and this consumer (only `sage26_set_disk_scale_radius` writes it, at pre-timestep), so the target's field is the saved value. The recheck saves and passes its own. The bin is the substep's, `(substep * 10) / num_substeps` capped at 9. The post-minor-merger recheck runs when `sage26_disk_instability` is configured as `process_by_galaxy` (SAGE26's `DiskInstabilityOn`); its black-hole growth runs when `sage26_quasar_mode` is configured as `process_per_event` (SAGE26's `AGNrecipeOn`), with `BlackHoleGrowthRate` and `QuasarModeEfficiency`. `sage26_finalize_sfr_histories` averages the bulge history into `SfrBulge` and `SfrBulgeZ` after the last substep. The package's `StarFormationRate` diagnostic, which SAGE26 does not hold, adds `stars / dT` with the snapshot interval.

## Properties

- Reads: `Type`, `HaloNr`, `SnapNum`, `dT`, `Mvir`, `Vvir`, `Vmax`, `ColdGas`, `MetalsColdGas`, `StellarMass`, `MetalsStellarMass`, `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass`, `MergerBulgeMass`, `InstabilityBulgeMass`, the three bulge radii (`BulgeSizeOn` 3), `HotGas`, `MetalsHotGas`, `EjectedGas`, `MetalsEjectedGas`, `BlackHoleMass`, `QuasarModeBHaccretionMass`, `DiskScaleRadius`, `UnstableDiskGasFraction`, and with `SFprescription 1` `H2gas` and `H1gas`
- Writes: `ColdGas`, `MetalsColdGas`, `StellarMass`, `MetalsStellarMass`, `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass`, `MergerBulgeMass`, `InstabilityBulgeMass`, `HotGas`, `MetalsHotGas`, `EjectedGas`, `MetalsEjectedGas`, `StarFormationRate`, `SupernovaOutflowRate`, the bulge history elements `SfrBulgeBin<k>`, `SfrBulgeColdGasBin<k>`, `SfrBulgeColdGasMetalsBin<k>` of the substep's bin, and, through the post-minor-merger recheck's black-hole growth, `BlackHoleMass` and `QuasarModeBHaccretionMass`; with `BulgeSizeOn 3`, the three bulge radii (the instability channel's updates); with `SFprescription 1`, `H2gas` and `H1gas` (the commits' caps, the refresh and the recheck's black-hole cap)

## Parameters

- `FeedbackReheatingEpsilon`
- `FeedbackEjectionEfficiency`
- `RecycleFraction`
- `Yield`
- `FracZleaveDisk`
- `StarFormingDiskFactor`
- `BlackHoleGrowthRate`
- `QuasarModeEfficiency`
- `ThresholdMajorMerger`
- `BulgeSizeOn` (required, 0 or 3): SAGE26's bulge-size mode. 0, SAGE26's R0, computes no sizes (`R/src/model_misc.c:242-247`, `:395`); 3, the run files' value, is SAGE26's Tonini et al. (2016) merger and instability bulges (`:317-424`, `R1.par:109`). Modes 1 and 2 are not ported: any value other than 0 or 3, or a missing key, fails initialisation with an error naming the parameter. Mode 3 also reads the core's `Hubble_h`.
- `SFprescription` (required, 0 or 1): the prescription the burst's commits test for their hydrogen caps (`R/src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:1131`, `:1169`), its refresh tests (`R/src/model_mergers.c:798`) and the recheck's black-hole growth tests for its cap (`:366-367`); 1 is the run files' value (BR06, SAGE26's R2), 0 SAGE26's R0 and R1. Any other value, or a missing key, fails initialisation with an error naming the parameter.
- `StarburstColdGasOn` (required, 1): bursts use the cold gas (`R/src/model_mergers.c:680-683`), SAGE26's default (`R/src/core_read_parameter_file.c:109`); 0, the H2 burst fuel, is not ported and is refused, as is a missing key.
- `FIREmodeOn` (required, 0 or 1, no default; campaign decision D33): 1, the run files' value (SAGE26's R3, `R3.par:55`), runs every burst's FIRE budget (`R/src/model_mergers.c:690-752`); 0 (R0-R2) the fixed-epsilon budget. A missing key fails initialisation and any other value is refused, naming the parameter. This module builds the burst parameter bundle, so it passes `FIREmodeOn` and the run's redshift table (`ZZ`, `Snaplistlen`) to every burst it runs: the instability's, the merger's and the post-minor-merger recheck's. `sage26_calculate_supernova_feedback` reads the same parameter for the disk path.

## Ordering

**Enforced at init by event subscription validation (fails with ERROR if violated):**

1. When used as `process_per_event`, `sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption` must be configured as `process_full_halo` in the same substep phase — it is the only producer of `merger` events that this module consumes.

**Advisory (emits WARNING if violated):**

- `process_by_galaxy` mode: `sage26_disk_instability` should precede this module in the same substep phase — without it, `UnstableDiskGasFraction` is zero and the disk-instability starburst channel fires on no galaxies.
- `process_per_event` mode (when the post-merger disk-instability recheck is active): `sage26_quasar_mode` as `process_per_event` is needed for the recheck's black-hole growth, which SAGE26 runs before the recheck's burst.

## Events

Consumes `merger` events from `sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption`.

## Tests

`_tests/test_unit_sage26_starburst_feedback_oracle.c` runs every SAGE26 oracle fixture under `_tests/oracle/` through `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` directly, with the fixture's ratio, mode, channel, saved disk radius, burst host, FoF central, bin and substep `dt` standing in for the trigger and `BulgeSizeOn` 0 or 3 (the c07 `burst_s3_*` fixtures) and `SFprescription` 0 or 1 (the c08 `burst_r2_*` fixtures, which also assert the hydrogen record: the commits' caps, the refresh's flags, budget and fused remainder, and the oracle's observation of the post-minor-merger recheck's black-hole growth, whose capped flags and, where a cap binds, its bound are compared with the burst's `recheck_bh_caps` locals), and compares the result bit for bit with SAGE26's own routine; outside the oracle environment it reports SKIP. The recheck's black-hole cap is end-state invariant (the recheck's burst re-caps and refreshes the hydrogen), so those locals are its only evidence. The c09 `burst_fire_*` fixtures run with `FIREmodeOn` 1 (or 0, the `burst_fire0_*` twin) and R3's flags: the test gives the bundle the fixture's `FIREmodeOn` and redshift table and also asserts every galaxy's `MassLoading` unchanged, the burst's FIRE flags and values against the oracle's record, and the FIRE wiring of the burst and, where the oracle observes SAGE26's recheck burst taking the FIRE branch, of the recheck's own burst: each read its host's stored `SnapNum`, float `Vvir` and that snapshot's redshift. `_tests/test_unit_sage26_starburst_feedback.c` asserts the refresh's platform-independent invariants, the module's wiring of `SFprescription 1` into the burst at module level, and that the recheck's black-hole growth receives the burst's `SFprescription` (`test_hydrogen_cap_recheck_growth`); and, for `FIREmodeOn`, the burst's FIRE budget against SAGE26's expressions written inline and against `FIREmodeOn 0` on the same input with `MassLoading` unwritten, the FIRE wiring of each burst path (the direct burst, the merger and instability channels through `process()`, and the post-minor-merger recheck's burst), the parameter's refusal and D35's refusal through a burst.
