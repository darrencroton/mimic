# `sage26_starburst_feedback`

Ports SAGE26's collisional starburst, `collisional_starburst_recipe` (`src/model_mergers.c:584-831` in the staged SAGE26 source), with `StarburstColdGasOn 1`, `BulgeSizeOn` 0 or 3, `SFprescription` 1 (the run files' value, SAGE26's R2, kept through R6) or 0 (R0, R1) and `FIREmodeOn` 1 (the run files' value, SAGE26's R3: the FIRE supernova budget) or 0 (R0-R2: fixed-epsilon), and `CGMrecipeOn` 1 (the run files' value, SAGE26's R4, kept through R6: regime-aware routing) or 0 (R0-R3), for disk-instability triggers and merger events. With `SFprescription 1` the burst's star-formation and feedback commits cap the hydrogen (`shared/sage_sf_feedback_physics.h`), the burst refreshes it after its feedback (`R/src/model_mergers.c:798-803`, step 7b below), and the post-minor-merger recheck's black-hole growth applies its accretion cap (`:366-372`, `shared/sage_agn_physics.h`).

## The Burst Transaction

One shared function, `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` (`shared/sage_starburst_physics.h`), owns SAGE26's whole sequence for both channels:

1. The bursting fraction: the trigger itself for a disk instability (`mode 1`) and `0.56 * ratio^0.7` for a merger (`mode 0`), as SAGE26's code has them (`:600-604`; its comment at `:577` has the modes reversed).
2. The burst gas is the host's total `ColdGas` (`StarburstColdGasOn 1`), floored at zero, times the fraction; the stars are floored at zero (`:680-688`).
3. The reheated mass. With `FIREmodeOn 1` (`shared/sage_fire_feedback_physics.h`): the Muratov et al. (2015) scaling from the burst **host's** (the target's, SAGE26's `merger_centralgal`) own float `Vvir` widened to double and the redshift of its **stored** snapshot, `ZZ[SnapNum]` (`:690-703`), never the satellite's or the FoF central's, nor the current halo snapshot's; then `capped_eta_reheat(FeedbackReheatingEpsilon * scaling, Vvir) * stars` (`:706-710`, the cap `2 * EtaSNcode * EnergySNcode / Vvir^2` in double), and, unlike the disk path, **no** `MassLoading` write. A stored `SnapNum` outside the run's redshift table is refused with an error naming it (`mimic_sage_fire_stored_redshift`, `shared/sage_fire_feedback_physics.h`), where SAGE26 reads `ZZ[SnapNum]` with no bounds check (`src/model_mergers.c:694`): the burst writes nothing and `process()` fails (a refusal in the post-minor-merger recheck's burst, step 8, comes after the outer burst's commits). With `FIREmodeOn 0`: `FeedbackReheatingEpsilon * stars` (`:711-712`). Both are scaled down together when they exceed the burst gas, with no positive-sum clause (`:720-725`).
4. The ejected mass from the burst host's own `Vvir` (`:727-749`), not the FoF central's: with `FIREmodeOn 1` the Hirschmann et al. (2016) energy-based ejection `(E_FB - E_lift) / (0.5 * Vvir^2)` when `E_FB = coupling * 0.5 * stars * EtaSNcode * EnergySNcode` exceeds `E_lift = 0.5 * reheated * Vvir^2`, from the renormalised stars and reheated mass, the velocity widened to double (`:729-736`, `sn_energy_coupling` capped at 2); with `FIREmodeOn 0` the fixed-epsilon formula with the float `Vvir` squared in float (`:738-743`). Zero for a non-positive `Vvir` in both, then floored at zero (`:746-748`).
5. The bulge history is sampled for every burst, zero stars included: `SfrBulge[bin] += stars / dt`, `SfrBulgeColdGas[bin] += ColdGas`, `SfrBulgeColdGasMetals[bin] += MetalsColdGas` (`:754-757`).
6. The star-formation commit (`update_from_star_formation`), then `recycled_stars = (1 - RecycleFraction) * stars` into `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass` (one fused multiply-add, `:773`) and the channel the caller selects, `MergerBulgeMass` or `InstabilityBulgeMass` (`:770-782`). The instability channel is followed at once by the incremental instability-bulge radius from the caller's saved disk radius, the transaction's `old_disk_radius` argument (`:783-784`, `shared/sage_bulge_size_physics.h`; a no-op with `BulgeSizeOn 0`); the merger channel writes no radius.
7. The feedback commit (`update_from_feedback`), reheated gas and ejection going to the FoF central (`:787-791`): to its CGM pair when `CGMrecipeOn` is 1 and the CENTRAL's `Regime` is 0, to its hot pair otherwise (`R/src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:1175-1247`), whatever the burst host's own `Regime`.
7b. With `SFprescription 1` (`sf_prescription_tracks_h2`, `:798`), the hydrogen refresh, which is not a pair of independent caps: `H2gas` is capped at the double `ColdGas * 0.74` (the widened float compared with the unfused product, which is narrowed on a binding store, `:799-800`); then `H1gas` is overwritten, whatever it held, with the atomic remainder `ColdGas * 0.74 - H2gas` (one fused multiply-subtract in SAGE26's object, written `fma(ColdGas, 0.74, -H2gas)`, `:801-802`), and a negative stored result is floored at zero (`:803`). It applies no ionisation correction, so an `H1gas` the disk star formation reduced for ionisation is restored to the full atomic remainder until the next disk SF; every burst runs it, a zero-star burst included, before the recheck. After the feedback commit's float cap of the same cold gas, the refresh's `H2gas` cap binds only where that float bound rounded up above the double product, and then stores the same float.
8. For a merger burst whose ratio is strictly below `ThresholdMajorMerger`, the post-minor-merger `check_disk_instability` on the host, whose gas branch grows the black hole (with its quasar wind and, for `SFprescription 1`, its hydrogen cap) and bursts again in `mode 1` into the instability channel, that burst refreshing the hydrogen in turn (`:806-811`) and, with `FIREmodeOn 1`, running the FIRE budget with the same host's `Vvir` and stored snapshot (it receives the same parameter bundle).
9. Only then the burst's metal yield: retained in the host's cold gas (less the fraction leaving the disk) when the host keeps more than `1e-8` of cold gas and the ratio is strictly below the threshold, otherwise all of it escapes (`:813-830`); the escaping metals go to the FoF central's selected metals through `add_metals_to_hot_reservoir` (`R/src/model_misc.h:67-74`): its `MetalsCGMgas` with `CGMrecipeOn 1` and the central's `Regime` 0, its `MetalsHotGas` otherwise. A ratio equal to the threshold is a minor merger in SAGE26 but gets no recheck and sends all its yield out. The recheck's quasar wind (step 8) selects the burst HOST's pair by the host's own `Regime` (`R/src/model_mergers.c:406-440`).

There is no early return on a non-positive trigger, zero cold gas or zero stars. The star-formation and feedback commits are the package's one implementation, `shared/sage_sf_feedback_physics.h`.

## Channels

- `process_by_galaxy`: the disk instability (`UnstableDiskGasFraction > 0`, `mode 1`, the instability channel), after `sage26_disk_instability` and `sage26_quasar_mode`, as SAGE26's `check_disk_instability` grows the black hole and then bursts (`src/model_disk_instability.c:101-108`). `dt` is the galaxy's own `dT / num_substeps` (`src/core_build_model.c:516`). It never acts on a feedback-free-burst row (`FFBRegime` 1): SAGE26's FFB call runs no instability check (`src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:1004-1008`, `:1365-1367`), and `sage26_disk_instability` writes nothing on that row, so its `UnstableDiskGasFraction` keeps its per-snapshot reset value 0.
- `process_per_event`: the merger event (`mode 0`), after `sage26_quasar_mode`'s merger black-hole growth. `dt` is the merging satellite's substep interval, carried in the event's `value1` (`src/core_build_model.c:579-581`, `src/model_mergers.c:285`, `:809`). The bulge channel is SAGE26's (`src/model_mergers.c:266-282`): `MergerBulgeMass` for a major merger (ratio strictly above `ThresholdMajorMerger`), and for a minor merger `InstabilityBulgeMass` onto a target that was disk-dominated before the satellite was added and `MergerBulgeMass` otherwise, from the flag the resolver carries on the target in `MergerDiskDominated` (`:251-254`). Every merger bursts, a ratio of 0 included, on an FFB target as on any other, its post-merger recheck included: SAGE26's merger routines do not read `FFBRegime`.

Each channel passes SAGE26's `old_disk_radius`: the galaxy's current `DiskScaleRadius` for the disk instability, as `check_disk_instability` passes it (`src/model_disk_instability.c:106-107`), and the target's `DiskScaleRadius` for a merger, which SAGE26 saves before `add_galaxies_together` (`src/model_mergers.c:257`); no package module writes `DiskScaleRadius` between that point of the resolver's transaction and this consumer (only `sage26_set_disk_scale_radius` writes it, at pre-timestep), so the target's field is the saved value. The recheck saves and passes its own. The bin is the substep's, `(substep * 10) / num_substeps` capped at 9. The post-minor-merger recheck runs when `sage26_disk_instability` is configured as `process_by_galaxy` (SAGE26's `DiskInstabilityOn`); its black-hole growth runs when `sage26_quasar_mode` is configured as `process_per_event` (SAGE26's `AGNrecipeOn`), with `BlackHoleGrowthRate` and `QuasarModeEfficiency`. `sage26_finalize_sfr_histories` averages the bulge history into `SfrBulge` and `SfrBulgeZ` after the last substep. The package's `StarFormationRate` diagnostic, which SAGE26 does not hold, adds `stars / dT` with the snapshot interval.

## Properties

- Reads: `Type`, `HaloNr`, `SnapNum`, `dT`, `Mvir`, `Vvir`, `Vmax`, `ColdGas`, `MetalsColdGas`, `StellarMass`, `MetalsStellarMass`, `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass`, `MergerBulgeMass`, `InstabilityBulgeMass`, the three bulge radii (`BulgeSizeOn` 3), `HotGas`, `MetalsHotGas`, `CGMgas`, `MetalsCGMgas`, `Regime` (the FoF central's for the feedback and yield, the host's for the recheck's wind), `EjectedGas`, `MetalsEjectedGas`, `BlackHoleMass`, `QuasarModeBHaccretionMass`, `DiskScaleRadius`, `UnstableDiskGasFraction`, `MergerDiskDominated` (the merger channel's), and with `SFprescription 1` `H2gas` and `H1gas`
- Writes: `ColdGas`, `MetalsColdGas`, `StellarMass`, `MetalsStellarMass`, `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass`, `MergerBulgeMass`, `InstabilityBulgeMass`, `HotGas`, `MetalsHotGas`, `CGMgas`, `MetalsCGMgas`, `EjectedGas`, `MetalsEjectedGas`, `StarFormationRate`, `SupernovaOutflowRate`, the bulge history elements `SfrBulgeBin<k>`, `SfrBulgeColdGasBin<k>`, `SfrBulgeColdGasMetalsBin<k>` of the substep's bin, and, through the post-minor-merger recheck's black-hole growth, `BlackHoleMass` and `QuasarModeBHaccretionMass`; with `BulgeSizeOn 3`, the three bulge radii (the instability channel's updates); with `SFprescription 1`, `H2gas` and `H1gas` (the commits' caps, the refresh and the recheck's black-hole cap)

## Parameters

Each parameter below is required with no default: a missing key, or a value outside the accepted values, fails initialisation. The three marked as conditional are read only under their condition and are not needed otherwise.

- `FeedbackReheatingEpsilon`: finite and >= 0, the range every reader shares (`mimic_sage_validate_feedback_reheating_epsilon`, `shared/sage_sf_feedback_physics.h`); SAGE26 does not range-check it
- `FeedbackEjectionEfficiency`: [0, 10]
- `RecycleFraction`: [0, 1]
- `Yield`: [0, 1]
- `FracZleaveDisk`: [0, 1]
- `StarFormingDiskFactor`: [0, 10]; conditional, read only when `sage26_disk_instability` is configured as `process_by_galaxy` in a substep phase (the post-minor-merger recheck's `Mcrit` factor)
- `BlackHoleGrowthRate`: (0, 1]; conditional, read only when `sage26_disk_instability` is configured as `process_by_galaxy` and `sage26_quasar_mode` as `process_per_event`, each in a substep phase (the recheck's black-hole growth)
- `QuasarModeEfficiency`: [0, 1]; conditional, under the same condition as `BlackHoleGrowthRate` (the recheck's wind)
- `ThresholdMajorMerger`: [0, 1]
- `BulgeSizeOn` (required, 0 or 3): SAGE26's bulge-size mode. 0, SAGE26's R0, computes no sizes (`R/src/model_misc.c:242-247`, `:395`); 3, the run files' value, is SAGE26's Tonini et al. (2016) merger and instability bulges (`:317-424`) and SAGE26's default (SAGE26's `src/core_read_parameter_file.c:107`; its `input/millennium.par` does not set it), which configurations R1–R6 use. Modes 1 and 2 are not ported: any value other than 0 or 3, or a missing key, fails initialisation with an error naming the parameter. Mode 3 also reads the core's `Hubble_h`.
- `SFprescription` (required, 0 or 1): the prescription the burst's commits test for their hydrogen caps (`R/src/model_starformation_and_feedback.c:1131`, `:1169`), its refresh tests (`R/src/model_mergers.c:798`) and the recheck's black-hole growth tests for its cap (`:366-367`); 1 is the run files' value (BR06, SAGE26's R2), 0 SAGE26's R0 and R1. Any other value, or a missing key, fails initialisation with an error naming the parameter.
- `StarburstColdGasOn` (required, 1): bursts use the cold gas (`R/src/model_mergers.c:680-683`), SAGE26's default (`R/src/core_read_parameter_file.c:109`); 0, the H2 burst fuel, is not ported and is refused, as is a missing key.
- `FIREmodeOn` (required, 0 or 1, no default): 1, the run files' value and SAGE26's default (SAGE26's `input/millennium.par:55`), which configurations R3–R6 set, runs every burst's FIRE budget (`R/src/model_mergers.c:690-752`); 0 (R0-R2) the fixed-epsilon budget. A missing key fails initialisation and any other value is refused, naming the parameter. This module builds the burst parameter bundle, so it passes `FIREmodeOn` and the run's redshift table (`ZZ`, `Snaplistlen`) to every burst it runs: the instability's, the merger's and the post-minor-merger recheck's. `sage26_calculate_supernova_feedback` reads the same parameter for the disk path.
- `CGMrecipeOn` (required, 0 or 1, no default): 1, the run files' value (SAGE26's R4, kept through R6), routes every burst's feedback and escaping yield by the FoF central's `Regime` and the recheck's wind by the host's; 0 (R0-R3) uses the hot pair throughout. A missing key fails initialisation and any other value is refused, naming the parameter. The module carries it in the burst parameter bundle to every burst, the recheck's included.

## Ordering

**Enforced at init (fails with ERROR if violated):**

1. When used as `process_per_event`, `sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption` must be configured as `process_full_halo` in the same substep phase — it is the only producer of `merger` events that this module consumes. `sage26_starburst_feedback_init()` refuses this itself too: when the module is `process_per_event` in a substep phase and no substep phase holds both it (`process_per_event`) and the resolver (`process_full_halo`).
2. When this module and `sage26_quasar_mode` are both `process_per_event` in one substep phase, `sage26_quasar_mode` must come first; `sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption_init()` refuses the other order (SAGE26 grows the black hole before the merger burst, `src/model_mergers.c:262-287`).

**Advisory (emits WARNING if violated; the run continues):**

- `process_by_galaxy` mode: `sage26_disk_instability` should precede this module in the same substep phase — without it, `UnstableDiskGasFraction` is zero and the disk-instability starburst channel fires on no galaxies. The warning is logged when this module is `process_by_galaxy` in a substep phase and no substep phase holds `sage26_disk_instability` (`process_by_galaxy`) before it (`process_by_galaxy`).
- Whenever `sage26_disk_instability` is `process_by_galaxy` in a substep phase (so the post-minor-merger recheck is active) and `sage26_quasar_mode` is not `process_per_event` in any substep phase, whatever this module's own modes: the recheck then skips its black-hole growth, which SAGE26 runs before the recheck's burst.

**Not refused or warned about:** the position of this module's `process_by_galaxy` entry relative to `sage26_quasar_mode`'s (SAGE26 grows the black hole before the instability burst, `src/model_disk_instability.c:102-108`); either mode alone; and the `process_per_event` entry without `sage26_disk_instability`, in which case no merger burst runs the post-minor-merger recheck (SAGE26's `DiskInstabilityOn` 0 at `src/model_mergers.c:807`).

## Events

Consumes `merger` events from `sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption`.

## Tests

`_tests/test_unit_sage26_starburst_feedback_oracle.c` runs every SAGE26 oracle fixture under `_tests/oracle/` through `mimic_apply_collisional_starburst` directly, with the fixture's ratio, mode, channel, saved disk radius, burst host, FoF central, bin and substep `dt` standing in for the trigger and `BulgeSizeOn` 0 or 3 (the `burst_s3_*` fixtures, "The c07 Group") and `SFprescription` 0 or 1 (the `burst_r2_*` fixtures, "The c08 Group", which also assert the hydrogen record: the commits' caps, the refresh's flags, budget and fused remainder, and the oracle's observation of the post-minor-merger recheck's black-hole growth, whose capped flags and, where a cap binds, its bound are compared with the burst's `recheck_bh_caps` locals), and compares the result bit for bit with SAGE26's own routine; outside the oracle environment it reports SKIP. The recheck's black-hole cap is end-state invariant (the recheck's burst re-caps and refreshes the hydrogen), so those locals are its only evidence. The `burst_fire_*` fixtures ("The c09 Group") run with `FIREmodeOn` 1 (or 0, the `burst_fire0_*` twin) and configuration R3's flags: the test gives the bundle the fixture's `FIREmodeOn` and redshift table and also asserts every galaxy's `MassLoading` unchanged, the burst's FIRE flags and values against the oracle's record, and the FIRE wiring of the burst and, where the oracle observes SAGE26's recheck burst taking the FIRE branch, of the recheck's own burst: each read its host's stored `SnapNum`, float `Vvir` and that snapshot's redshift. `_tests/test_unit_sage26_starburst_feedback.c` asserts the refresh's platform-independent invariants, the module's wiring of `SFprescription 1` into the burst at module level, and that the recheck's black-hole growth receives the burst's `SFprescription` (`test_hydrogen_cap_recheck_growth`); and, for `FIREmodeOn`, the burst's FIRE budget against SAGE26's expressions written inline and against `FIREmodeOn 0` on the same input with `MassLoading` unwritten, the FIRE wiring of each burst path (the direct burst, the merger and instability channels through `process()`, and the post-minor-merger recheck's burst), the parameter's refusal and, through a burst, the refusal of a stored `SnapNum` outside the run's redshift table; and, for `CGMrecipeOn`, `test_cgm_burst_module_channels` (both channels through `process()`, so the bundle's value: the feedback and the escaping yield in the FoF central's selected pair, the host in the other `Regime`, `CGMrecipeOn` 1 against 0 on the same input, exact or inline-SAGE26 expectations, the unselected pairs unchanged), `test_cgm_burst_recheck_sites` (the recheck's wind by the host's `Regime`, the outer and nested bursts' feedback and yield by the central's) and `test_cgm_recipe_on_refused`.

`_tests/test_integration_sage26_starburst_feedback.py` runs the module as `process_by_galaxy` in `galaxy_physics`, after `sage26_disk_instability`, alone, or at the end of the infall, reincorporation, cooling and disk-instability chain (with `sage26_reionization`, `sage26_prepare_infall_budget` and `sage26_set_disk_scale_radius` at pre-timestep), with `SFprescription` 0, `CGMrecipeOn` 0, `FIREmodeOn` 0 and `BulgeSizeOn` 0. It checks that each run succeeds; that `StellarMass`, `BulgeMass`, `ColdGas`, `HotGas`, `EjectedGas` and `StarFormationRate` are in the output, finite and non-negative; that `BulgeMass` stays within a stated rounding bound above `StellarMass` that grows with the number of bursts a galaxy can have; that a higher `FeedbackReheatingEpsilon` gives at least as much hot gas and a higher `FeedbackEjectionEfficiency` at least as much ejected gas; that the full chain produces gas; and that the run reports no memory leak and finishes within 30 s.
