# `sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption`

SAGE26's per-substep merger and disruption pass and its two transactions. Once per substep, after every galaxy's physics, the pass walks the FoF's rows in order; each satellite it removes mutates its target at once, so a later satellite in the same pass sees the earlier changes. A merging satellite runs SAGE26's `deal_with_galaxy_merger`, a disrupted one its `disrupt_satellite_to_ICS`.

## The Pass

`sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption_pass()` reproduces SAGE26's pass (`src/core_build_model.c:519-587`, `B` below). For each row, in order, a Type 1 or 2 not yet removed (`B:523`; a removed row is the package's Type 3):

1. Its clock must be below 999 (`B:524-527`, where SAGE26 aborts; a NaN clock aborts too). The module errors there.
2. Its clock is decremented by its substep interval `deltaT / N`, the float clock less the double interval (`B:529-530`). `deltaT` is the row's `dT`, which the core forms as SAGE26 does, `Age[SnapNum] - Age[current]`.
3. It is eligible when its float `StellarMass + ColdGas` is exactly zero, or positive with the interpolated virial mass `Mvir - deltaMvir * (1 - (step + 1) / N)` at most `ThresholdSatDisruption` times it (`B:534-537`). The interpolation is not floored at zero (the package's earlier floor is gone): an orphan's `Mvir` is 0 and its `deltaMvir` minus its previous mass, so it reaches 0 at the last substep. SAGE26's objects fuse it into one multiply-subtract, written here as `fma()`.
4. Its target is the FoF central for a Type 1 and its subhalo's central (`CentralHalo`) for a Type 2; a target already removed this snapshot is redirected once through its own `CentralHalo` (`B:539-543`, `shared/central_link.h`). A Type 1's `CentralHalo` is itself, so an orphan whose Type 1 has just merged lands on that merged galaxy, as in SAGE26. There is no fallback to the FoF central for an invalid link (the package's earlier one is gone); an index outside the workspace, a target equal to the satellite or a target without a galaxy is reported as an error rather than read, defences no valid run reaches (the core sets every `CentralHalo` inside its subhalo's slice).
5. A satellite with a non-finite clock stays as it is (`B:547`). Otherwise, at the event time `Age[SnapNum] - (step + 0.5) * (deltaT / N)` (`B:549`, fused in SAGE26's objects, written as `fma()`; `Age[SnapNum]` is the core's `Age[current]` (`ctx->time`) plus the row's `dT`, which the core forms as `Age[SnapNum] - Age[current]`), a positive clock disrupts it to the target's ICS (`B:574-575`) and any other clock merges it into the target, with the substep's history bin and `deltaT / N` (`B:576-581`).

A satellite that is not eligible, or whose clock is not finite, stays and is reconsidered at the next substep. There is no early orphan purge and no forced clock: an orphan's removal is an outcome of the pass. SAGE26's `mergeIntoID` (`B:545`), the output index of the target, has no package member (Mimic's output writes no merger target).

## The Disruption

`sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption_disrupt()` runs `mimic_sage_disrupt_satellite_to_ics()` (`shared/sage_merger_physics.h`), SAGE26's `disrupt_satellite_to_ICS` (`src/model_mergers.c:845-902`): the satellite's cold, hot and CGM gas and their metals, each summed in float and only then widened, to the PASSED target's selected pair through SAGE26's `add_gas_to_hot_reservoir` (`src/model_misc.h:76-85`, `shared/sage_cgm_routing.h`): its CGM pair with `CGMrecipeOn 1` and the target's own `Regime` 0, its hot pair otherwise. The target is the pass's `merger_centralgal` (step 4), for an orphan its subhalo's central, which need not be the FoF central; its ejected gas and existing ICS transferred; its stars and stellar metals into the target's ICS; positive stars booked into `ICS_disrupt`, with `StellarMass * time` into `ICS_sum_mt` (one fused multiply-add in SAGE26's objects, written as `fma()`); its black hole into the target's; a positive existing ICS booked into `ICS_accrete` with its `ICS_sum_mt`. Then exactly SAGE26's satellite fields are zeroed (`M:891-900`: the gas, ejected, ICS, ICS bookkeeping, stellar and bulge masses with their metals, and the black hole), and not the two bulge channels, the radii, `H1gas`, `H2gas`, `QuasarModeBHaccretionMass`, the outflow rate, the timestamps or the histories. The satellite becomes Type 3 (SAGE26's `mergeType` 4), reported to the action hook as `"disrupt"`.

## The Merger Transaction

`sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption_merge()` reproduces `deal_with_galaxy_merger` with `BulgeSizeOn` 0 or 3 (`src/model_mergers.c:228-333`, `M` below), in its order, from the pieces in `shared/sage_merger_physics.h` and `shared/sage_bulge_size_physics.h`:

1. The mass ratio from the float `StellarMass + ColdGas` sums of satellite and target, compared in float and widened into doubles; both sums zero gives 0 (`M:235-249`).
2. The target's morphology before the addition: disk-dominated when its float disk mass exceeds half its `StellarMass` (`M:253-254`).
3. `add_galaxies_together` (`M:456-543`): the cold, stellar, hot and ejected reservoirs with their metals; the ICS, a positive satellite ICS booked into `ICS_accrete` with its `ICS_sum_mt` and the satellite's `ICS_disrupt`, `ICS_accrete` and `ICS_sum_mt` cleared; the black hole; the CGM gas; the satellite's stars and metals into the bulge, its `InstabilityBulgeMass` and `MergerBulgeMass` into the target's, and its remaining disk stars into the target's instability channel when the target's disk fraction after the addition exceeds 0.5 (followed, with `BulgeSizeOn 3`, by the target's incremental instability-bulge radius from its own `DiskScaleRadius`, `M:516-522`), else its merger channel; and every bin of the satellite's disk and bulge histories into the target's bulge histories. The satellite's other reservoirs are left as they are.
4. The `merger` event, for every merger, a ratio of 0 included. Its consumers run immediately, in the phase's YAML order: `sage26_quasar_mode` (`grow_black_hole`, `M:262-264`) and then `sage26_starburst_feedback` (the burst, `M:266-287`), which selects its bulge channel from the morphology of step 2, carried on the target in `MergerDiskDominated`.
5. `get_bulge_radius` on the target (`M:293`), then the remnant radius (`M:297`): SAGE26's static `calculate_merger_remnant_radius` (`M:135-212`, `mimic_sage_merger_remnant_radius()`), computed from the target after the addition, the black hole and the burst, and from the satellite, which still holds its reservoirs. Each progenitor's mass is its float `StellarMass + ColdGas`; its radius the mass-weighted half-mass radius of its disk (1.68 times `DiskScaleRadius`) and bulge (`BulgeRadius`) when that sum is positive, else 0; a non-positive radius takes the other's, R1 first, and two give 0; the self-binding, orbital and radiative (Covington et al. 2011) energies are doubles, and a non-positive total falls back to the mass-weighted radius. SAGE26's object fuses three multiply-adds here (`M:158`, `:171`, `:204`, the oracle README's c07 table), written as `fma()`. With `BulgeSizeOn 0` this `get_bulge_radius`, and each one below, zeroes the three radii (`src/model_misc.c:242-247`), so the remnant radius never reaches the state and the module does not form it.
6. A ratio strictly above `ThresholdMajorMerger` is a major merger: `make_bulge_from_burst` (`M:553-570`; `BulgeMass` and `MergerBulgeMass` take the final `StellarMass`, `InstabilityBulgeMass` is zeroed, `MetalsBulgeMass` takes `MetalsStellarMass`, the disk histories move into the bulge histories), then `MergerBulgeRadius` takes the remnant radius (a float store), `get_bulge_radius`, and `TimeOfLastMajorMerger` (`M:299-312`). `DiskScaleRadius` is not zeroed. Every other merger, a ratio of 0, at or below 0.1, or equal to the threshold included, sets `TimeOfLastMinorMerger`; onto a target that was disk-dominated before the addition (step 2) it then only runs `get_bulge_radius`, discarding the remnant radius, and onto a spheroid-dominated one it stores the remnant radius into `MergerBulgeRadius` and runs `get_bulge_radius` (`M:313-331`). Both timestamps are the event time, a lookback time in code units, written out times `UnitTime_in_Megayears`, -1 (never) included.
7. The satellite becomes Type 3 (SAGE26's `mergeType` 2 or 1), reported to the action hook as `"merge"`.

## Processing Contract

- Supported mode: `process_full_halo`
- Expects the full halo workspace for the current FoF system
- Emits the `merger` event (declared under `events.emits` in `module_info.yaml`): `value0` the mass ratio, `value1` the satellite's substep `dt`; the target carries `MergerDiskDominated`

## Ordering

**Enforced at init (fails with ERROR if violated):**

1. `sage26_initialise_merger_clock` must be in `pre_timestep` as `process_full_halo` — without it, satellites carry the sentinel `MergTime` value (999.9) from the tree load and the module hard-errors on the first satellite it encounters at runtime.
2. When `sage26_quasar_mode` and `sage26_starburst_feedback` are both merger-event consumers of one phase, the quasar consumer must come first: SAGE26 grows the black hole before the merger burst (`M:262-287`).

## Properties

- Reads: `Type`, `Mvir`, `deltaMvir`, `dT`, `SnapNum`, `CentralHalo`, `MergTime`, the disruption target's `Regime` (`CGMrecipeOn 1`) and every reservoir, channel and history the two transactions move
- Writes: `MergTime`, `Type` (3 after a merger or disruption); in a merger, the target's reservoirs, `BulgeMass`, `MetalsBulgeMass`, `MergerBulgeMass`, `InstabilityBulgeMass`, `ICS_accrete`, `ICS_sum_mt`, `CGMgas`, `MetalsCGMgas`, the three bulge radii, the six histories, `MergerDiskDominated`, `TimeOfLastMinorMerger` and `TimeOfLastMajorMerger`, and the satellite's `ICS_disrupt`, `ICS_accrete` and `ICS_sum_mt`; in a disruption, the target's `HotGas`, `MetalsHotGas` or `CGMgas`, `MetalsCGMgas` (its selected pair), `EjectedGas`, `MetalsEjectedGas`, `ICS`, `MetalsICS`, `ICS_disrupt`, `ICS_accrete`, `ICS_sum_mt` and `BlackHoleMass`, and the satellite's fields SAGE26 zeroes

## Parameters

- `ThresholdMajorMerger`
- `ThresholdSatDisruption`
- `BulgeSizeOn` (required, 0 or 3): SAGE26's bulge-size mode. 0, SAGE26's R0, computes no sizes (`R/src/model_misc.c:242-247`, `:395`); 3, the run files' value, is SAGE26's Tonini et al. (2016) merger and instability bulges (`:317-424`, `R1.par:109`), with the remnant radius and its major/minor assignment after the burst (`M:293-330`). Modes 1 and 2 are not ported: any value other than 0 or 3, or a missing key, fails initialisation with an error naming the parameter. Mode 3 also reads the core's `Hubble_h`.
- `CGMrecipeOn` (required, 0 or 1, campaign decision D33): 1 (SAGE26's R4) deposits a disruption's gas in the target's pair its `Regime` selects; 0, the run files' value (R0-R3), in its hot pair. A merger's addition moves CGM to CGM and hot to hot either way (`M:464-465`, `:496-497`). Any other value, or a missing key, fails initialisation with an error naming the parameter.

## Tests

- `_tests/test_unit_sage26_resolve_mergers_and_disruption_oracle.c` compares the module with SAGE26's own routines and the oracle's source-reviewed transcriptions bit for bit on every fixture under `_tests/oracle/`: `add_galaxies_together` (with `BulgeSizeOn 3` for the c07 `add_s3_*` fixtures) and `make_bulge_from_burst` through the shared helpers directly; `deal_with_galaxy_merger` through the module's transaction and its real event consumers in order, zero-ratio mergers included, with `BulgeSizeOn 3` for the c07 `merge_s3_*` fixtures (the synchronisation, the remnant radius with its locals and every branch, and the three assignments); `disrupt_satellite_to_ICS` through the disruption entry; the pass on each pass fixture, with every row's pass record against SAGE26's driver locals and the merger events in the pass's order (several mergers in one bin included); and joining (the core's inheritance, `sage26_initialise_merger_clock` and `sage26_set_disk_scale_radius`) followed by the pass, which shows a new orphan keeping its histories and moving them into its target's bulge histories in a merger. The c08 `merge_r2_*` and `tr_pass_r2_*` fixtures run the transaction and the pass with `SFprescription 1` (R2), so the consumers' black-hole cap, the burst's commit caps and hydrogen refresh and the nested recheck's run, and chain two mergers in one bin; the end state is asserted bit for bit. The merger's black-hole cap is end-state invariant (the burst's commit re-caps and its refresh overwrites `H1gas`), so the test runs the shared growth beside the quasar consumer on a copy of the target with the fixture's `SFprescription`, requires the copy to equal the consumer's result bit for bit, and asserts that copy's cap flags and bound against the oracle's `bh_*` record and `max_h_bh`; the burst's hydrogen record is consumed, the burst oracle test asserting it at its routine boundary. The c09 `merge_fire_*` and `tr_pass_fire_*` fixtures (routines `deal_with_galaxy_merger_fire` and `merger_pass_fire`, R3's flags, a target whose `Vvir` and stored `SnapNum` differ from the satellite's and the FoF central's, and two chained mergers in one bin) run the burst consumer with the fixture's `FIREmodeOn` and redshift table: the test replays the consumer's call of the shared transaction on copies beside it, requires the replay to equal the consumer's result bit for bit, and asserts its FIRE wiring (the merger burst read the TARGET's own stored `SnapNum`, float `Vvir` and that snapshot's redshift) and, for the transaction, its FIRE record against the oracle's and the recheck burst's wiring where the oracle observes that burst taking the FIRE branch; every galaxy's `MassLoading` must stay as it was. The burst consumer gets its required `FIREmodeOn` on every fixture (0 before c09). Outside the oracle environment it reports SKIP.
- `_tests/test_unit_sage26_merger_ordering_parity.c` checks the pass's immediate ordering and its one-hop redirect, and that the merger transaction with `BulgeSizeOn 3` refuses a context without the core parameters (no `Hubble_h`) with -1, before changing any galaxy or emitting its event. `test_cgm_disruption_to_passed_recipient` runs the pass at `CGMrecipeOn` 1 against 0 on the same input: an orphan disrupted into its subhalo's Type 1 central (not the FoF central, which holds the other `Regime`) adds its exact float gas and metal sums to that recipient's selected pair, the unselected pair and the FoF central unchanged; `test_cgm_recipe_on_refused` checks the parameter's refusals.
- `_tests/test_integration_sage26_merger_event_consumers.py` checks the event chain end to end, the written timestamps against `UnitTime_in_Megayears`, and the merger bulge bounds.
